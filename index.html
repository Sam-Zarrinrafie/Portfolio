Profile

Electrical engineering grounded in practical development.

I am an Electrical Engineering student at York University and a Robotics and Electronics Lead Engineer at STEM MINDS, where I worked in product development on Farmtron. My projects have taken systems from early mechanical and electrical prototypes through integrated embedded software and physical testing.

My research interests include radar, wireless communications, sensing, resilient systems, and practical uses of machine learning and language models.

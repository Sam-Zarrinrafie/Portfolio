01

Product development · STEM MINDS CORP

Farmtron

An automated CoreXY farming system.

I developed Farmtron’s electronics, embedded control, and software as part of my product-development work at STEM MINDS. The system combined a CoreXY gantry and vertical toolhead with watering, soil probing, and automatic tool changes.

The work connected custom PCB design and physical prototyping with a Linux touchscreen interface, AI-assisted commands, and remote operation.

  • Python
  • C/C++
  • Arduino UNO Q
  • Linux
  • PCB design
  • Cloudflare
01 / SENSING

Optical tool docking

Developed automatic alignment with two light sensors. Comparing LED-on and LED-off readings isolated the tracking signal; live graphs and simulation helped debug docking behavior.

02 / ELECTRONICS

Custom PCB & motion

Designed a PCB and integrated sensors, stepper motors, and pump control. Tested the electronics alongside firmware for coordinated motion, homing, and emergency stops.

03 / SOFTWARE

Linux & AI control

Built a Debian-based environment on Arduino UNO Q with a touchscreen interface. Connected an LLM and speech recognition to machine-control functions, with motor timing handled by microcontroller firmware.

04 / CONNECTIVITY

Remote operation

Developed authenticated commands, device pairing, and status monitoring through a Cloudflare-hosted service. Added signed update manifests, integrity checks, and software rollback support.

02

Independent project

Wireless robotic arm

Remote commands. Physical movement.

Built a multi-axis robotic arm with wireless remote control, servo-driven joints, and onboard power. Designed the assemblies in CAD and produced 3D-printed parts, adjusting component fit to accommodate servos, electronics, and wiring.

Connected wireless commands to Arduino-based servo control, then tested joint movement, power delivery, and mechanical fit. Repeated testing helped resolve wiring and assembly problems.

  • Arduino
  • Wireless control
  • Servos
  • CAD
  • 3D printing

02 · Independent Engineering Project

Wireless Mobile Manipulation Platform

I designed and built a remotely operated six-axis arm on a custom aluminum tracked chassis. A scale arm controller mapped the operator’s physical gestures to the full-size arm, while a purpose-built serial protocol carried joint, propulsion, camera, and safety commands through a modified Bluetooth link.

The platform combined seven high-torque servos, four independently driven tracks, distributed Arduino controllers, multi-rail power, active cooling, circuit protection, remote electrical interlocks, and a two-axis camera turret. I modified the HC-05 radio boards with SMA connectors and external directional and omnidirectional antennas to extend the control link beyond the modules’ original onboard antennas.

  • Arduino
  • Bluetooth
  • Serial protocols
  • Servo control
  • Power systems
